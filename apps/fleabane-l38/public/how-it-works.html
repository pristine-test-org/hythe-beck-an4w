How the shared list works

One list, kept by everyone who uses it. Here is what happens between adding an item and seeing it on the page.

  1. 1. Add an item Anyone can put a short title on the list. It appears at the top within moments.
  2. 2. Everyone sees it The page reads the five newest items each time it loads, so the list is always current.
  3. 3. Tick it off Finished items keep their place and gain a check mark, so nothing is lost.

Fleabane keeps the list deliberately small: five items, newest first, nothing hidden behind menus or settings.
